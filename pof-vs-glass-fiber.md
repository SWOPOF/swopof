# POF vs Glass Fiber

Plastic optical fiber (POF) and glass optical fiber are widely used in different communication and lighting systems.

## Plastic Optical Fiber Advantages

- Flexible
- Easy installation
- Lower cost
- Better for short distance communication
- Suitable for automotive ambient lighting

## Glass Fiber Advantages

- Longer transmission distance
- Higher bandwidth
- Better for telecom backbone

## Which One Should You Choose?

POF is ideal for:

- Automotive lighting
- Industrial short-distance communication
- Decorative lighting
- Sensor systems

Glass fiber is more suitable for long-distance telecom transmission.
